How to use Monedero

Monedero is a paycheck budget. Each time you get paid, you give every dollar a job, then track spending against that plan. Here's how it works.

Guide Support Privacy

The big idea

  • Ready to Assign

    Money in your accounts that doesn't have a job yet. Your goal is to bring it to $0.

  • Expenses

    Planned costs like rent, power, and your phone bill, kept in groups such as Bills or Food.

  • Budgets

    Flexible spending limits, like Gasoline or Eating Out, that show how much you have left.

Step 1

Set up in a few minutes

The first time you open Monedero, a short setup walks you through the basics:

  • Pay period: how often you get paid. Weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month, monthly, or a custom schedule.
  • Account: add the account your money lives in, like Checking, with its current balance.
  • Group and expenses: create a group like Bills and add your first expenses.

Everything after the pay period is optional. You can skip ahead and add more later.

Step 2

Plan your paycheck

The Plan tab is where you budget each pay period. At the top, Ready to Assign shows the money that still needs a job. Income you record lands here automatically.

  • Tap Assign to see every budget and expense in one list, then tap one to give it money.
  • Use the arrows at the top to move between pay periods.
  • When Ready to Assign reaches $0, every dollar has a job.
If you assign more than you have, Ready to Assign turns red and shows a Fix button to help you pull money back.

Step 3

Track bills and expenses

Below your budgets, Plan lists your groups and the expenses inside them. Each row shows what you've spent, what you assigned, and what's left. A red bar means you've gone over.

  • Tap the + at the top of Plan to add an expense, a budget, or a group.
  • Tap an expense to change its amount, name, icon, group, or due date.
  • Turn on Recurring for bills that repeat every month, and Cleared once a bill is paid.
  • Touch and hold a row, then drag it to reorder.

Step 4

Add transactions

Tap the big + in the middle of the tab bar any time you spend or receive money.

  • Choose Expense or Income, type the amount and merchant, and pick the account.
  • Pick a Category to file it to an expense or budget. If you leave it empty, it shows up as Un-Planned.
  • Use Pay Card to record a credit card payment, or Transfer to move money between accounts.
  • Tap More to change the date.
Monedero remembers your merchants. Start typing one you've used before and tap the suggestion to fill in its category too.

Step 5

See the big picture on Home

Home shows your current pay period, how many days are left, and a breakdown chart with three views:

  • Planned: what you've assigned to each group this period.
  • Budgets: how much is still available in each budget.
  • Un-Planned: spending that isn't filed to an expense or budget yet. Tap one to file it.

Tap a row to see its details, or tap the gear to open Settings.

Step 6

Review your activity

The Activity tab lists every transaction by day, with the total spent this period at the top.

  • Use the arrows to see earlier months.
  • Tap the magnifying glass to search, or the filter button to narrow the list.
  • Tap a transaction to edit it. Swipe left to delete it, and tap Undo if you change your mind.

Step 7

Keep your accounts in sync

The Accounts tab shows each account's balance, grouped into checking, savings, credit cards, and cash. Credit cards show what you owe.

  • Tap + to add an account.
  • Tap an account to see its balance, its cleared balance, and its transactions.
  • Tap Edit on an account to rename it or change its details.

Step 8

Make it yours

Tap the gear on Home to open Settings.

  • Pay Period: change your pay schedule any time.
  • Roll Over Leftover Money: carry money you didn't assign into the next period's Ready to Assign.
  • Clear Budgets Each Pay Period: start each new period with no budgets instead of carrying them over.
  • Reminders: a daily nudge to log spending, alerts when a pay period starts or ends, and a heads-up the day before a bill is due.
  • App Icon: pick the icon you like best.

Quick tips

Add the widget

See your budgets at a glance. Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Monedero.

Never miss a bill

Give expenses a due date and keep Upcoming Expense Alerts on. Tap an alert to jump straight to that bill in Plan.

Clear out Un-Planned

Un-Planned spending comes out of Ready to Assign. File it to an expense or budget so your plan stays accurate.

Private by design

Your budget stays on your iPhone. No account, no bank login, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
